METHODS (to adapt): LUCAS Topsoil 2018 points for Romania with a valid pH(H2O) value (n = 602; one per 396 km2; coordinates: TH_LAT/TH_LONG) and the ECHO samples were assigned to 30 x 30 km cells of the Stereo 70 grid. Cells with at least 2 ECHO samples and 1 LUCAS point were retained. Agreement was expressed as the Spearman correlation between the ECHO and LUCAS cell medians, as the linearly weighted kappa for three pH classes (<6.0, 6.0-7.5, >7.5) fixed beforehand, and as the share of cells in the same class; intervals are 95% percentile intervals from a bootstrap that resamples 100-km blocks. To separate local from regional agreement, the correlation was recomputed after centring both medians within 100-km blocks.

RESULTS (to adapt): 136 cells contained both ECHO and LUCAS data (553 ECHO samples, 244 LUCAS points; 136 of 365 ECHO cells). Cell medians of ECHO and LUCAS pH were correlated (Spearman rho = 0.38 (95% CI 0.14 to 0.51); n = 136 cells). Weighted kappa for the three pH classes was 0.30 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.39); 59% of the cells fell in the same class and 96% in the same or an adjacent class. The median difference between ECHO and LUCAS cell medians was -0.52 (95% CI -0.81 to -0.33) pH units (descriptive, not an error estimate). After centring within 100-km blocks the correlation was 0.21 (95% CI 0.03 to 0.47).

SENSITIVITY S1_ge2_collectors: n = 74 cells; rho = 0.35 (95% CI 0.10 to 0.49); kappa = 0.29 (95% CI 0.09 to 0.46).

SENSITIVITY S2_ge3echo_ge2lucas: n = 30 cells; rho = 0.20 (95% CI 0.02 to 0.52); kappa = 0.24 (95% CI 0.00 to 0.56).
