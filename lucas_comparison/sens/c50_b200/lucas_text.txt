METHODS (to adapt): LUCAS Topsoil 2018 points for Romania with a valid pH(H2O) value (n = 602; one per 396 km2; coordinates: TH_LAT/TH_LONG) and the ECHO samples were assigned to 30 x 30 km cells of the Stereo 70 grid. Cells with at least 2 ECHO samples and 1 LUCAS point were retained. Agreement was expressed as the Spearman correlation between the ECHO and LUCAS cell medians, as the linearly weighted kappa for three pH classes (<6.0, 6.0-7.5, >7.5) fixed beforehand, and as the share of cells in the same class; intervals are 95% percentile intervals from a bootstrap that resamples 100-km blocks. To separate local from regional agreement, the correlation was recomputed after centring both medians within 100-km blocks.

RESULTS (to adapt): 90 cells contained both ECHO and LUCAS data (1034 ECHO samples, 571 LUCAS points; 90 of 108 ECHO cells). Cell medians of ECHO and LUCAS pH were correlated (Spearman rho = 0.55 (95% CI 0.32 to 0.68); n = 90 cells). Weighted kappa for the three pH classes was 0.36 (95% CI 0.11 to 0.49); 59% of the cells fell in the same class and 100% in the same or an adjacent class. The median difference between ECHO and LUCAS cell medians was -0.53 (95% CI -0.84 to -0.37) pH units (descriptive, not an error estimate). After centring within 100-km blocks the correlation was 0.33 (95% CI 0.13 to 0.46).

SENSITIVITY S1_ge2_collectors: n = 77 cells; rho = 0.55 (95% CI 0.34 to 0.70); kappa = 0.41 (95% CI 0.19 to 0.56).

SENSITIVITY S2_ge3echo_ge2lucas: n = 76 cells; rho = 0.53 (95% CI 0.28 to 0.69); kappa = 0.32 (95% CI 0.07 to 0.47).
